01 · STORY

Preserving the Dignity of the Royal Table

Sura-sang was the table set before the Joseon kings each day. Only one kind of food could be placed upon it: the ‘real’ dishes made from carefully selected ingredients and true dedication. We carry that same spirit, serving our guests ‘soy-marinated crab fit for the royal table.’

Ganjang gejang has long been treated as a prized dish — from the royal sura-sang of the Joseon court to an essential centerpiece of today's fine Korean dining. Sura Gejang honors that tradition, serving every meal with the dignity of a small palace in the heart of the city.

02 · INGREDIENTS

Only Seasonal Female Crab from Yeonpyeong Island

The flavor of ganjang gejang comes down to the freshness of the crab itself. We use only seasonal female crab caught in the waters off Yeonpyeong Island, hand-selecting each one for its rich roe through a strict screening process. Preserving that traditional flavor, no matter the season or catch, is our guiding principle.

03 · TASTE

A Full Table, a True Rice Thief

Deeply aged ganjang gejang meets freshly steamed rice to create a true ‘rice thief’ — the dish so good it makes you eat bowl after bowl. Add 1++ Korean beef yukhoe, spicy sura crab stew, and seafood pancake, and you have a hearty table that goes far beyond a single plate of crab.
